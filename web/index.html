Class recordings

Every class is recorded and lands in Panopto. You reach it through Canvas → Panopto Video, or by the links in the course repository's Classes/README.md. Two things go wrong and both are fixable: you must be on the Canvas roster, and you must have signed in to Panopto at least once for access to attach. If a link appears to do nothing, that is almost always why — tell the teaching assistant rather than assuming you are locked out.
