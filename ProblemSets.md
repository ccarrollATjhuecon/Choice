# Problem Sets

**Problem sets are posted in the private repository for the year you are taking the
course** — `ccarrollATjhuecon/Choice-YYYY`, under `ProblemSets/`. If you are taking the
course in 2026, that is
[`ccarrollATjhuecon/Choice-2026`](https://github.com/ccarrollATjhuecon/Choice-2026).

That repository is private, so you have to be invited into it. **Send your GitHub username
to the teaching assistant** and you will be added. Make the account with your JHU address
and apply for [GitHub Education](https://github.com/education/students), which is free and
worth having. Until your username reaches the TA, none of the problem sets will open for
you.

## Due dates are on the calendar, and only there

**The [course calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=jhuecon.org_v2kso2hp1memk0vka1gomvorm8%40group.calendar.google.com)
is the single authoritative source for when a problem set is due.** The teaching assistant
puts each due date there. If a date written in a problem set, sent in an email, or said
aloud in class disagrees with the calendar, **the calendar is right** — those other places
are copies, and copies go stale.

Subscribe to it, and you will not have to keep track of this yourself:

- [Add to Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0?cid=amh1ZWNvbi5vcmdfdjJrc28yaHAxbWVtazB2a2ExZ29tdm9ybThAZ3JvdXAuY2FsZW5kYXIuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbQ)
- [Subscribe by iCal](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/ical/jhuecon.org_v2kso2hp1memk0vka1gomvorm8%40group.calendar.google.com/public/basic.ics) (Apple Calendar, Outlook)

Not every deadline for the term goes up at once. The calendar tells you what is next.

## Answers

Answer keys appear in the same folder as the problem set, once the deadline has passed —
never before it.
